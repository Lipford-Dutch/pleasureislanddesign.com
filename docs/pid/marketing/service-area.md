# Service area: the one list the site uses

Updated 2026-11-02 in the website audit fixes PR.

## The list

The site names these **six towns** wherever it lists where we work. Each one has its own location page:

| Town | Page |
|---|---|
| Wilmington | `locations/wilmington.html` |
| Carolina Beach | `locations/carolina-beach.html` |
| Kure Beach | `locations/kure-beach.html` |
| Wrightsville Beach | `locations/wrightsville-beach.html` |
| Leland | `locations/leland.html` |
| Burgaw | `locations/burgaw.html` |

Always use this order: Wilmington, Carolina Beach, Kure Beach, Wrightsville Beach, Leland, Burgaw. It goes in:

- the homepage schema `areaServed`
- the homepage trust list
- the FAQ answer
- the service-area subtitle
- the "Communities We Serve" list

The wider wording is "surrounding coastal NC communities" or "SE North Carolina".

## Neighborhoods

These are neighborhoods inside the six towns, not separate service areas:

- Leland: Brunswick Forest and Compass Pointe
- Wilmington: Porters Neck, Mayfaire, Landfall and Ogden

They can stay in the "Communities We Serve" list and in gallery captions.

## Why this list

Before the audit, the homepage gave three different lists:

- The schema and the trust list had Compass Pointe and Hampstead.
- The FAQ and the subtitle had Southport.
- None of the lists had Burgaw, even though Burgaw has its own page.

Across the site the six location-page towns came up far more than anything else. Wilmington appeared in about 180 places, and Burgaw, the lowest of the six, in about 60. Southport (3) and Hampstead (2) appeared only on the homepage.

## Waiting on Will

Southport and Hampstead were taken out of the lists but not ruled out. If we really work in them regularly, add them back everywhere at once, ideally with a location page for each.
