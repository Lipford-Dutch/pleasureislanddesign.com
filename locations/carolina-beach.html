Your Carolina Beach Cabinet Specialists

Carolina Beach is home — the heart of Pleasure Island and where our studio has served neighbors for over a decade. Carolina Beach FAQ

Common Questions

Questions about our service in the Carolina Beach area? Don't see yours below? Contact us directly — we're happy to help.

Do you serve all of Carolina Beach?

Yes — Carolina Beach is our home turf. We serve all of Pleasure Island including Wilmington Beach, Hanby Beach, the Boardwalk district, and the canal and lake neighborhoods, with no travel fees. Call (910) 444-1230 and we'll confirm your street right away.

How far do you travel for Carolina Beach projects?

Our primary service area covers all of Pleasure Island, greater Wilmington, and the Cape Fear coast. We are based nearby in Wilmington and do not charge travel fees for Carolina Beach projects. For projects further out, contact us and we'll work something out.

How soon can you start my Carolina Beach project?

Lead times vary with the season. Spring and summer are our busiest periods across the region, so we recommend reaching out 4–6 weeks in advance if possible. During slower seasons, we can sometimes start within 1–2 weeks of your consultation. Request a consultation to get a current availability estimate.

Do you offer free estimates in Carolina Beach?

Absolutely. All consultations and estimates are completely free and come with no obligation. We'll visit your home, assess your cabinets, discuss your vision, and provide a detailed written quote — all at no cost to you.

What is the process for a Carolina Beach project?

After your free consultation, we schedule your project start date. We arrive, carefully remove and tag every cabinet door, protect your kitchen surfaces, and take the doors to our dedicated spray booth facility. We complete in-home frame work simultaneously. Most Carolina Beach projects are fully complete and cabinets reinstalled in 5–8 business days.
