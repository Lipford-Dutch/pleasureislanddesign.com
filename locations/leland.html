Leland FAQ

Common Questions

Questions about our service in the Leland area? Don't see yours below? Contact us directly — we're happy to help.

Do you serve all of Leland?

Yes — we serve all of Leland and greater Brunswick County, including Brunswick Forest, Compass Pointe, Magnolia Greens, Waterford, Mallory Creek, and Westgate. Call (910) 444-1230 and we'll confirm your community right away.

How far do you travel for Leland projects?

Our primary service area covers Leland and greater Brunswick County, all of Pleasure Island, and greater Wilmington. We are based nearby in Wilmington and do not charge travel fees for Leland projects. For projects further out, contact us and we'll work something out.

How soon can you start my Leland project?

Lead times vary with the season. Spring and summer are our busiest periods across the region, so we recommend reaching out 4–6 weeks in advance if possible. During slower seasons, we can sometimes start within 1–2 weeks of your consultation. Request a consultation to get a current availability estimate.

Do you offer free estimates in Leland?

Absolutely. All consultations and estimates are completely free and come with no obligation. We'll visit your home, assess your cabinets, discuss your vision, and provide a detailed written quote — all at no cost to you.

What is the process for a Leland project?

After your free consultation, we schedule your project start date. We arrive, carefully remove and tag every cabinet door, protect your kitchen surfaces, and take the doors to our dedicated spray booth facility. We complete in-home frame work simultaneously. Most Leland projects are fully complete and cabinets reinstalled in 5–8 business days.
