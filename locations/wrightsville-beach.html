Common Questions

Do you serve all of Wrightsville Beach?

Yes — we serve all of Wrightsville Beach, including Harbor Island, Shell Island, the Loop, and the soundfront and oceanfront neighborhoods. Call (910) 444-1230 to confirm your street.

How far do you travel for Wrightsville Beach projects?

Our primary service area covers Wrightsville Beach, greater Wilmington, and all of Pleasure Island. We are based nearby in Wilmington and do not charge travel fees for Wrightsville Beach projects. For projects further out, contact us and we'll work something out.

How soon can you start my Wrightsville Beach project?

Lead times vary with the season. Spring and summer are our busiest periods across the region, so we recommend reaching out 4–6 weeks in advance if possible. During slower seasons, we can sometimes start within 1–2 weeks of your consultation. Request a consultation to get a current availability estimate.

Do you offer free estimates in Wrightsville Beach?

Absolutely. All consultations and estimates are completely free and come with no obligation. We'll visit your home, assess your cabinets, discuss your vision, and provide a detailed written quote — all at no cost to you.

What is the process for a Wrightsville Beach project?

After your free consultation, we schedule your project start date. We arrive, carefully remove and tag every cabinet door, protect your kitchen surfaces, and take the doors to our dedicated spray booth facility. We complete in-home frame work simultaneously. Most Wrightsville Beach projects are fully complete and cabinets reinstalled in 5–8 business days.
